READING THE LEDGER

A recent view.
A careful comparison.

This is the latest accepted report per species at each exact eBird hotspot in a rolling 30-day source window. The shorter filters use the saved capture’s date, not today.

These counts are not historical or seasonal totals. Reporting effort, checklist coverage, review status and eBird’s sensitive-species restrictions affect what appears. A notable flag means eBird returned that species in the separate notable feed; it is not our assessment of local rarity. The notable flag always refers to the full 30-day source capture, even when viewing a shorter date window.

For seasonal patterns and the full historical bird list, open the hotspot on eBird.
